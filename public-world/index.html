DATA TRUST BY DESIGN

We separate demonstration data from verified facts

The platform is built around source tracking, review status and freshness. Missing facts stay missing rather than being filled by AI.

✓ Verified records

  • Require a source URL or accepted supplier evidence
  • Carry verification and effective dates
  • Can be reviewed again when sensitive facts change

⚑ Demo records

  • Exist only to demonstrate the product experience
  • Are clearly labeled and never presented as real partnerships
  • Do not contain invented rankings, commissions or admission odds
